# miras.commons results: replication

The severe-stress E1 (ρ = 0.7, 1,952 cells × 50 runs) with master seeds 1, 2 and 3 (October 2026, miras 0.2.1). Seed 1 is the ρ = 0.7 half of the full E1; seeds 2 and 3 were run with `--seed 2` and `--seed 3`. The curated findings are in `commons-findings.md`.

Generated by `miras commons analyze`; regenerate with:

```
miras commons analyze commons_output/e1_summary.csv \
    commons_output/e1-rho0.7-seed2_summary.csv commons_output/e1-rho0.7-seed3_summary.csv --rho 0.7
```


Runs: `e1`, `e1-rho0.7-seed2`, `e1-rho0.7-seed3`. Numbers are averages over cells of 50-run means (see each summary's `reps`); they are conditional on the model's calibration (specification amendment A3).
